How I work

Four principles that hold across every system below.

M / 01

Build > Study

I learn by building. A concept becomes a working end-to-end system, not a set of notes. Practice over theory, every time.

M / 02

Iterate

Once something has my real interest, I don't stop until it's right. The hard part is the first step — after that I iterate like a ritual until it's actually solid.

M / 03

AI in the Loop

I don't lean on one all-purpose AI. I split the work by role — ideation, documentation, building, auditing, final review — each handled by a different model, with me directing and owning every call.

M / 04

Rigor

I care about catching edge cases, validating AI output, and keeping systems auditable instead of black-box.

~/systems/mc_tft.py

MC-TFT — Multi-Camera Target Face Tracking System

B.Tech Major Project

Real-time pipeline detecting, recognizing, and tracking a target person across 4 simultaneous, non-overlapping CCTV streams via face embeddings and cosine similarity matching. Compared MTCNN, RetinaFace, and YOLOv8-Face on structured evaluation and selected RetinaFace. Resolved occlusion-induced identity drift, low-light embedding mismatches, and multi-stream latency under load. Built a Flask dashboard for live monitoring.

~/systems/jobshield.py

JobShield — AI Agent for Job Posting Scam Detection

AMD Developer Hackathon, ACT II — Unicorn Track

Multi-signal agent verifying job postings against WHOIS lookups, live web search, typosquat detection, and LinkedIn presence. A deterministic, code-level risk-scoring layer — not overridable by model judgment — keeps fraud decisions auditable. Orchestrates deepseek-v4-flash for extraction and deepseek-v4-pro for synthesis behind a Streamlit UI, deployed with Docker.
